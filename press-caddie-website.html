Built for how you actually play

Every side bet, actually tracked

Not a scorecard app that happens to add up money. Press Caddie is built around the bet first — handicaps, presses, and settlement all live in the same round.

01 — Live scoring

Everyone enters their own

Each player submits their own score, hole by hole. The host confirms before it locks in — so nobody's rewriting history after a bad back nine.

02 — Real settlement

Know exactly who owes who

Every format nets out automatically into the minimum number of payments. Confirm it's paid, right in the app, before the next round can start.

03 — Real handicaps

Course-accurate, not eyeballed

Real course ratings and slope, pulled from an actual golf course database — not a guess at how many strokes you're really getting.
